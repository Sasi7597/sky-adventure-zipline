About Sky Adventure's

A Big Adventure
Above the Valley

Sky Adventure's is built around one simple idea: combine the thrill of a long zipline ride with the beauty of the surrounding landscape. The 1 KM experience is designed to give visitors a memorable outdoor adventure with clear guidance and a strong focus on safety.
